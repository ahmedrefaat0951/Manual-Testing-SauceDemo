## Test Account

`problem_user`

## Preconditions

- User is logged in using the `problem_user` account.
- User is on the Products page.
- The Sauce Labs Backpack, Sauce Labs Bike Light, and Sauce Labs Onesie have been successfully added to the cart.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Locate the Sauce Labs Backpack, Sauce Labs Bike Light, and Sauce Labs Onesie on the Products page.
2. Click **Remove** for each product.
3. Observe the button state for each product.
4. Observe the cart counter displayed in the header.

## Expected Result

- Each product's button should change from **Remove** back to **Add to Cart**.
- The cart counter should decrease accordingly, reaching 0 after all three products are removed.

## Actual Result

- All three buttons remained in the **Remove** state after being clicked.
- The cart counter remained at 3 instead of decreasing to 0.

## User Impact

Users may be unable to remove unwanted products directly from the Products page, making cart management less convenient. However, they can still remove products from the Cart page, providing a workaround. This reduces the severity of the issue, but still affects usability and may cause confusion.
