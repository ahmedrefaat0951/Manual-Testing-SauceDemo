## Test Account

`problem_user`

## Preconditions

- User is logged in using the `problem_user` account.
- User is on the Products page.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Locate the six products displayed on the Products page.
2. Click **Add to Cart** for each product.
3. Observe the button state for each product after clicking.
4. Observe the cart counter displayed in the header.

## Expected Result

- All six selected products should be successfully added to the cart.
- Each product's button should change from **Add to Cart** to **Remove**.
- The cart counter should display **6**.

## Actual Result

Only three of the six products were successfully added to the cart.

The Add to Cart buttons for the **Sauce Labs Backpack**, **Sauce Labs Bike Light**, and **Sauce Labs Onesie** changed to **Remove**, while the buttons for the remaining three products remained as **Add to Cart**.

The cart counter displayed **3** instead of the expected 6.

## User Impact

Users may be unable to add certain products to their carts, preventing them from purchasing those items. This can lead to abandoned purchases, lost sales, and reduced customer trust.
