## Preconditions

- User is logged in using the `problem_user` account.

## Steps to Reproduce

**Scenario 1 — Products Page**

1. Navigate to the **Products** page.
2. Locate the six displayed products.
3. Click the title of each product individually.
4. Compare the clicked product title with the title of the resulting **Product Details** page.
5. Return to the **Products** page and repeat the process for the remaining products.

**Scenario 2 — Cart Page**

1. From the **Products** page, add the three products that can be successfully added to the cart.
2. Navigate to the **Cart** page.
3. Click the title of each product individually.
4. Compare the clicked product title with the title of the resulting **Product Details** page.
5. Return to the **Cart** page and repeat the process for the remaining products.

## Expected Result

- Clicking a product title should navigate to that product's corresponding **Product Details** page.
- The displayed product title and details should match the product selected, regardless of whether navigation starts from the **Products** page or the **Cart** page.

## Actual Result

Clicking product titles navigated to incorrect **Product Details** pages from both the **Products** and **Cart** pages.

**Products Page Results**

| Product title clicked | Resulting page |
|---|---|
| Sauce Labs Backpack | Sauce Labs Fleece Jacket |
| Sauce Labs Bike Light | Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt |
| Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt | Sauce Labs Onesie |
| Sauce Labs Fleece Jacket | ITEM NOT FOUND |
| Sauce Labs Onesie | Test.allTheThings() T-Shirt (Red) |
| Test.allTheThings() T-Shirt (Red) | Sauce Labs Backpack |

**Cart Page Results**

| Product title clicked | Resulting page |
|---|---|
| Sauce Labs Backpack | Sauce Labs Fleece Jacket |
| Sauce Labs Bike Light | Sauce Labs Bolt T-Shirt |
| Sauce Labs Onesie | Test.allTheThings() T-Shirt (Red) |

The same incorrect destination mappings were observed for these three products from both pages.

## User Impact

Users may be redirected to incorrect product pages when attempting to view product details from either the **Products** or **Cart** page. This can cause confusion, reduce trust in the website, and lead users to view incorrect product descriptions and prices when considering a purchase.
